Hi! Here's how to install Miracles on your iPhone. It takes 5 minutes.


STEP 1 -- Install TestFlight

Open the App Store on your iPhone. Search for "TestFlight" (Apple's
official app for beta-testing apps). Tap the cloud icon to install.
The icon is white with a propeller-like blue logo.

If you already have TestFlight from another app, skip this step.


STEP 2 -- Accept the invite

You will get an email from Apple, subject something like
"Elisa Fazzari has invited you to test Miracles". Open that email
ON YOUR IPHONE (not on your computer).

Tap "View in TestFlight" or the redeem code button in the email.
TestFlight will open and ask you to accept. Tap "Accept".


STEP 3 -- Install Miracles

In TestFlight, you should now see "Miracles" listed.
Tap it, then tap "Install" (or "Update" if a new version is available).

When it finishes, the Miracles icon will appear on your home screen
just like any other app.


STEP 4 -- Open Miracles and grant permissions

Open Miracles. The first time you run it, the app will ask:

  - "Miracles Would Like to Send You Notifications" -- tap ALLOW.
    This is so you get notified when Elisa (DD) or Beat post things.

  - "Allow 'Miracles' to use your location?" -- tap ALLOW WHILE USING
    APP. This is so the family map shows where you are when the app
    is open. It does NOT track you in the background.

After that, you'll see "My Identity -- Who are you?". Tap your name
("Mom" or "Beat") to set yourself.

That's it -- you're in. Welcome.


GETTING UPDATES

Whenever I (DD) ship an update to the app, you will get an email
from TestFlight saying "Miracles -- New build available". Open
TestFlight on your phone and tap "Update" next to Miracles. Done.

You don't need to do anything else. No App Store, no developer mode,
nothing weird. Just TestFlight + Update.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

  - "I never got the invite email" -- check spam, then text DD.
  - "TestFlight says the build expired" -- happens after 90 days
    if I haven't pushed a new version. Text DD and I'll push one.
  - "Miracles crashes on launch" -- text DD, ideally with a
    screenshot of what you saw before the crash.
  - "I tapped Don't Allow on notifications by accident" -- open
    iPhone Settings -> Miracles -> Notifications -> turn it on.
